# Rocket Run 🚀

Deep space survival game! Thread your rocket through the asteroid belt, harvest energy cores to charge your shield, and survive as the void accelerates around you.

## 🎮 Play Live

Play the game directly in your browser:
[**Play Rocket Run**](https://rocket-run-livid.vercel.app)

## 🕹️ Controls

| Action | Key / Input |
| --- | --- |
| **Launch Game** | `Enter` or click **Launch** |
| **Steer Left / Right** | `←` / `→` or `A` / `D` (or touch/drag) |
| **Activate Shield** | `Space` |
| **Pause Game** | `P` or `Esc` |

## ✨ Features

- **Endless Space Survival**: Thread through dangerous asteroid fields as speed increases over time.
- **Energy Cores & Shields**: Harvest energy cores to charge your shield and protect your ship.
- **Responsive Controls**: Supports keyboard, mouse, and touch drag controls for mobile/desktop.
